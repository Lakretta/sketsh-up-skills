# Architectural Floor Plan Review & Audit Checklist

Use this systematic checklist whenever reviewing, auditing, or critiquing a floor plan (from a photo, PDF, CAD drawing, BTI plan, or SketchUp layout).

---

## 1. Entry & Circulation Audit

- [ ] **Isolated Dirty Zone (`Брудна зона`)**: Does the path from bedrooms to the bathroom or kitchen cross the entry doormat where street shoes are removed? *(If yes -> flag as a **Recommended Adjustment** or **Blocking Issue**; relocate bathroom door or enlarge entry tile zone).*
- [ ] **Entry Sightline (`Видова точка з порогу`)**: What is the first thing visible when the front door opens? Avoid direct sightlines into a toilet bowl, messy kitchen sink, or bed pillow. Aim for a console table, mirror, accent wall panel, or window axis.
- [ ] **Corridor Efficiency**: Are corridors narrower than `900 mm` (`1000 mm` if doors swing into them)? Does dead corridor space exceed `15%` of apartment area without serving as wardrobe storage?
- [ ] **Furniture Delivery Path**: Can large items (`2100 mm` sofa, `1800 × 2000 mm` mattress, `2750 mm` wardrobe side panels) physically turn corners from the front door into target rooms?

---

## 2. Door Swings & Collision Matrix

- [ ] **Door-to-Door Clashes**: Do any two adjacent doors collide when opened `90°` simultaneously?
- [ ] **Bathroom / WC Safety Rule**: Do bathroom, WC, and laundry room doors open **outward**? *(Inward-opening doors in compact bathrooms are a safety hazard if someone faints inside).*
- [ ] **Door-to-Cabinet / Appliance Clashes**:
  - Does a room door hit a wardrobe facade or prevent drawers from pulling out?
  - Does the refrigerator door open past `90°–115°` without hitting a wall so vegetable crisper drawers can be removed? *(Requires a `50–100 mm` filler strip `фальш-планка` next to a wall).*
  - Can the dishwasher door (`~650 mm` projection) drop down fully while standing in front of the sink without blocking the trash pull-out?

---

## 3. Built-In Storage & Niche Engineering Audit

- [ ] **Niche Depth Verification**:
  - Are built-in coat and bedroom wardrobe niches at least **`620–650 mm` deep** (plaster-to-plaster)? *(A `500 mm` niche forces awkward front-facing hangers).*
- [ ] **90-Degree Inside Corner Geometry**:
  - Does every built-in cabinet niche have a **`80–120 mm` door-frame nib (`простінок`)** so the door casing (`лиштва`) sits flat rather than butting into a wardrobe side?
- [ ] **Installation & Filler Clearances**:
  - Are wall-to-wall built-in cabinets designed with `15–30 mm` scribing filler strips (`добірні планки`) or `10–15 mm` assembly tolerance so panels fit between slightly uneven plastered walls?
- [ ] **Corner Cabinet Dead Zones**:
  - In L-shaped kitchens or wardrobes, will the handles of one door hit the adjacent perpendicular door? *(Requires a `35–50 mm` L-shaped corner filler strip).*

---

## 4. Wet Zones, Plumbing & Engineering Audit

- [ ] **Toilet Drain Slope (`Ø110 mm`)**:
  - Is the toilet within **`1000–1500 mm`** of the main sewage riser (`стояк каналізації`) without sharp `90°` bends? *(Each meter requires `15–20 mm` vertical drop).*
- [ ] **Greywater Drain Slope (`Ø50 mm` — Sink, Shower, Washer, Dishwasher)**:
  - Does the pipe route require chasing into load-bearing concrete walls (`несучі монолітні пілони` — strictly prohibited!) or raising the shower floor excessively (`20–30 mm` drop per meter)?
- [ ] **Access Hatches (`Ревізійні люки`)**:
  - Is there unobstructed access (`>= 400 × 600 mm`) to water meters, shut-off valves, filters, leakage protection (`Neptun / Ajax WaterStop`), boiler, and AC concealed units?
- [ ] **Ventilation & Exhaust**:
  - Does the kitchen hood exhaust route cleanly to the kitchen vent shaft without blocking the natural ventilation grille?

---

## 5. Acoustic, Daylight & Thermal Comfort Audit

- [ ] **Bedroom Acoustic Buffering**:
  - If a bedroom shares a wall with a bathroom, kitchen, elevator shaft, or neighbor's living room, is a **built-in wardrobe** or **acoustic framing (`50–70 mm` mineral wool + double gypsum)** placed along that wall?
- [ ] **Radiator & Window Curtain Pocket Clearance**:
  - Did the layout reserve **`150–250 mm`** along window walls for recessed ceiling curtain tracks (`ніша під карниз`) and radiators (`конвектори`) before starting a wardrobe or desk?
- [ ] **Air Conditioning (`Кондиціонер`) Placement**:
  - Never aim the indoor AC split unit directly at the **bed pillows**, **sofa seating spot**, or **work desk chair**. Place it above the doorway or over a circulation zone blowing parallel to the bed/sofa.

---

## 6. Output Format for Floor Plan Reviews

Always structure your audit report using these three sections:

1. **Blocking Issues (Critical — Fix Immediately)**:
   - List exact locations where doors collide, clearances fall below `< 550–600 mm`, appliances cannot open, or plumbing slopes fail.
2. **Recommended Adjustments (Ergonomics & Flow)**:
   - Practical adjustments with exact millimeter targets (e.g., *"Shift the bedroom doorway `150 mm` left to create a full `650 mm` deep niche for the ceiling-height wardrobe"*).
3. **Design Opportunities (Value-Add Enhancements)**:
   - High-impact architectural improvements (e.g., S-shaped shared partition niches between hallway and bedroom, full-height concealed doors, acoustic slatted panels).
