# Ergonomics, Anthropometrics & Spatial Clearances Reference

All dimensions are specified in **millimeters (`mm`)** and follow **Neufert**, **ASID/NCIDQ**, and **European / Ukrainian residential ergonomic standards**.

---

## 1. Universal Circulation & Doorway Standards

| Element / Zone | Minimum (`mm`) | Optimal (`mm`) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Entry Hallway Width** | `1100` | `1300–1500` | Plus `600–650 mm` for coat wardrobe depth |
| **Interior Corridor Width** | `900` | `1050–1200` | `>= 1000 mm` if doors open into the corridor |
| **Primary Room Circulation Path** | `850` | `900–1050` | Main traffic route through living/dining areas |
| **Secondary Passage (Single Person)** | `700` | `750–850` | Between furniture pieces without active pull-out |
| **Tight Squeeze / Side Access** | `550–600` | `650` | Side of bed or behind occupied dining chair |
| **Entry Door Clear Opening** | `850–900` | `900–950` | Standard structural opening `980–1000 × 2080 mm` |
| **Bedroom / Living / Kitchen Door** | `800` | `800–900` | Structural opening `880–900 × 2070 mm` |
| **Bathroom / WC / Pantry Door** | `700` | `700–800` | Must swing **outward** (or slide) for safety |

## 2. Entry Hallway (`Передпокій`)

- **Dirty Zone Footprint**: Minimum `1200 × 1200 mm` (optimal `1400 × 1600 mm`) hard-surface flooring (porcelain stoneware) immediately inside the entry door, isolated from living room/bedroom transit paths.
- **Outerwear Wardrobe Depth**:
  - Standard perpendicular coat hangers: **`600–650 mm` external depth** (`>= 550 mm` internal clear depth so jacket shoulders do not rub doors).
  - Narrow hallway (< `1500 mm` total width): Use **`400–450 mm` depth** with pull-out front-facing telescopic bars (`тромбон`).
- **Shoe Storage & Bench**:
  - Seating bench (`Пуф / Банкетка`): `W = 600–900 mm`, `D = 380–450 mm`, `H = 420–450 mm`.
  - Open under-shelf for daily wet shoes: `H = 180–220 mm` with hidden LED strip (`3000K`).
  - Clear dressing zone in front of bench/wardrobe: **`>= 850–900 mm`**.
- **Hallway Console / Drop Zone (`Консоль для ключів`)**:
  - Depth: `250–380 mm`, Height: `750–850 mm` (or wall-mounted floating console at `Z = 750–820 mm`).

---

## 3. Living Room & Media Zone (`Вітальня`)

- **Sofa Ergonomics**:
  - Standard sofa depth: `900–1050 mm` (lounge/modular deep sofas: `1050–1150 mm`); seat height: `400–450 mm`.
  - Distance between **Sofa and Coffee Table**: **`400–450 mm`** (close enough to reach a cup without standing, wide enough for knees and passage).
  - Distance between **Sofa/Chairs and TV Console**: Minimum `800 mm` passage if it is a walkway.
- **TV Viewing Distance & Mounting Height**:
  - Center of TV screen (`Z_center`): **`1000–1150 mm`** from finished floor (aligned with seated eye level).
  - Optimal viewing distance by diagonal (4K resolution):
    - `50" (127 cm)` -> `1900–2400 mm`
    - `55" (140 cm)` -> `2100–2700 mm`
    - `65" (165 cm)` -> `2500–3200 mm`
    - `75" (190 cm)` -> `2900–3700 mm`
    - `85" (216 cm)` -> `3300–4200 mm`
- **TV Console (`Тумба під ТВ`)**:
  - Width: At least **`300–600 mm` wider than the TV** (`150–300 mm` margin on each side) for visual balance (typically `W = 1600–2400 mm`).
  - Depth: `350–450 mm`.
  - Height: `400–520 mm` (for wall-hung consoles: bottom edge at `Z = 180–250 mm`, top surface at `Z = 450–550 mm`).

## 4. Kitchen & Dining (`Кухня та Столова зона`)

### A. Kitchen Work Triangle & Cabinet Geometry
- **Work Triangle Sequence**: **Fridge (Storage) -> Sink (Prep/Wash) -> Hob/Stove (Cooking)**.
  - Sum of 3 sides: **`3600–6500 mm`**; each leg between **`1200 mm` and `2700 mm`**.
  - Main prep surface (`Робоча поверхня`) must sit **between the Sink and the Hob**: minimum **`800 mm`** (optimal **`900–1200 mm`**) continuous countertop.
- **Minimum Side Clearances**:
  - Between Sink and Hob: `>= 600 mm` (never place directly adjacent).
  - Between Hob and tall cabinet / wall: `>= 300–450 mm` (fire & elbow clearance).
  - Landing zone next to Fridge / Oven: `>= 400 mm` countertop.
- **Vertical & Depth Dimensions**:
  - Lower base cabinets + worktop height: **`860–920 mm`** (calculated as `User_Elbow_Height - 100..150 mm`; for height `175–180 cm`, **`900–910 mm`** is optimal).
  - Plinth (`Цоколь`): `100–120 mm` high, recessed `40–50 mm`.
  - Worktop depth: **`600–650 mm`** (base carcass `530–560 mm` deep for ventilation and plumbing).
  - Backsplash height (`Фартух`): **`550–600 mm`** between worktop and upper cabinets.
  - Upper wall cabinets depth: **`300–350 mm`** (if creating a deep upper mezzanine row above `Z = 2100 mm`, upper mezzanine depth is `580–600 mm` flush with tall fridge/oven columns).
- **Aisle Width (Parallel / Island Kitchen)**:
  - Single cook: minimum **`1050 mm`**, optimal **`1200 mm`** (allows open dishwasher door `600–650 mm` + walking bypass).
  - Two cooks: **`1200–1400 mm`**.

### B. Dining Table & Chairs
- **Table Footprint per Person**: `600–700 mm` width × `400 mm` depth (plus `200 mm` center shared serving strip -> minimum table width `800–900 mm`).
  - 4 persons: `1200 × 800 mm` (or `Ø950–1050 mm`).
  - 6 persons: `1600–1800 × 900 mm` (or `Ø1200–1350 mm`).
  - 8 persons: `2200–2400 × 1000 mm`.
- **Chair Pull-Back Clearances (from table edge to wall/obstacle)**:
  - Seated only (no pass-through behind): `550–600 mm`.
  - Standing up / sliding out chair: **`750–800 mm`**.
  - Walking behind an occupied chair: **`950–1100 mm`**.

## 5. Bedroom & Wardrobe (`Спальня та Гардероб`)

- **Bed Dimensions (Mattress vs. Outer Frame)**:
  - Queen Double (`1600 × 2000 mm` mattress) -> Outer frame `1680–1760 × 2100–2200 mm`.
  - King Double (`1800 × 2000 mm` mattress) -> Outer frame `1880–1960 × 2100–2200 mm`.
  - Single / Teen (`900–1200 × 2000 mm` mattress) -> Outer frame `980–1280 × 2080–2150 mm`.
- **Clearances Around the Bed**:
  - Side passages (left & right for double bed): minimum **`600 mm`**, optimal **`700–850 mm`**.
  - Foot of the bed (if main passage): **`750–900 mm`**.
  - Bedside tables (`Тумби приліжкові`): `W = 450–600 mm`, `D = 380–450 mm`, `H = 450–550 mm` (flush with mattress top).
- **Bedroom Wardrobe Clearances**:
  - Hinged doors (`450–550 mm` leaf width) + standing space: minimum **`850–950 mm`** between wardrobe front and bed frame.
  - Internal pull-out drawers behind hinged doors: require **`900–1000 mm`** clear front space and a **`16–25 mm` internal spacer/false side** on the hinge side to clear `110°` hinges.
  - Ceiling-height wardrobes (`H > 2750 mm`): split vertically at **`Z = 2100–2200 mm`** into a lower main wardrobe + upper mezzanine (`Антресоль`) due to `2800 × 2070 mm` LDSP sheet limits.
- **Walk-In Closet (`Гардеробна кімната`)**:
  - One-sided shelving (`550–600 mm` storage + `800–900 mm` passage) -> minimum room width **`1400–1500 mm`**.
  - Two-sided parallel storage (`600 mm` + `850–950 mm` passage + `600 mm` or `450 mm`) -> minimum room width **`1900–2150 mm`**.

## 6. Bathroom & Sanitary Node (`Санвузол та Ванна кімната`)

- **Toilet (`Унітаз / Інсталяція`)**:
  - Concealed cistern installation frame (`Інсталяція`): adds **`150–200 mm` depth** (`W = 500 mm`, `H = 1150–1200 mm` ledge); bowl projection `500–540 mm`.
  - Centerline to side wall / vanity / shower glass: minimum **`400 mm`**, optimal **`450 mm`** (total width niche `>= 800–900 mm`).
  - Clear space in front of toilet bowl: minimum **`600 mm`**, optimal **`700–750 mm`**.
- **Washbasin & Vanity (`Тумба з умивальником`)**:
  - Width: single basin `600–1000 mm` (`450–500 mm` compact guest WC); double basin `1200–1600 mm`.
  - Depth: `450–550 mm`.
  - Top rim height (`Z_top`): **`850–900 mm`** from finished floor.
  - Clear standing zone in front of vanity: **`>= 700–850 mm`**.
- **Shower & Bathtub**:
  - Walk-in shower cabin: minimum **`900 × 900 mm`**, comfortable **`1000 × 1200 mm`** or **`900 × 1400 mm`**.
  - Bathtub: standard **`1700–1800 × 750–800 mm`**, `H = 550–600 mm`.
- **Laundry Tower (`Пральна + Сушильна машина`)**:
  - Niche internal clear width: **`640–660 mm`** (for `600 mm` appliances + vibration gap).
  - Niche internal clear depth: **`650–700 mm`** (accounting for rear hoses and cabinet doors).

## 7. Home Office & Study (`Робоче місце`)

- **Desk Dimensions**:
  - Width: minimum `1000 mm`, optimal **`1200–1600 mm`**.
  - Depth: minimum `600 mm` (laptop), optimal **`700–800 mm`** (external `27"–34"` monitor so eyes sit `600–750 mm` from screen).
  - Height: **`730–750 mm`** fixed (or `650–1250 mm` sit-stand motorized frame).
- **Chair Clearance**:
  - Minimum **`900–1000 mm`** behind desk edge for rolling office chair and standing up.
  - Position desk **perpendicular to the window** (with natural light from the left for right-handed users) to prevent screen glare and silhouette backlighting on video calls.
